## 3. Commercial opportunity and platform proposition

Develop a **site-care operations and client reporting platform**. Supervisors record scheduled tasks and inspections, attach approved evidence, flag defects and issue a simple monthly report to each client. Begin with one service (such as landscaping) and one site; expand to cleaning and plant care after the process is proven.

### Recommended platform modules

- **Site register** — Client, property/site, service scope, access requirements and assigned supervisor.
- **Recurring task schedules** — Weekly/monthly maintenance calendar with task checklists.
- **Mobile work logs** — Completed tasks, timestamp, responsible team, notes and photos.
- **Issue/defect register** — Record irrigation faults, damaged assets, access problems or cleaning issues with ownership and due dates.
- **Client dashboard** — View approved schedules, completed work, open issues and monthly summary.
- **Sustainability metrics** — Optional water, waste, plant health or chemical-use reporting only where reliable data is collected.

### Suggested site structure

```text
/
/services/
/sectors/
/projects/
/request-a-site-assessment/
/client-portal/
/about/
/contact/
/operations/ (private)
```

### Priority user journey

Estate manager/developer → request assessment → receive scope and recurring schedule → supervisor records site work → issues are flagged and resolved → client receives a monthly evidence-based report. Keep staff workflow mobile-first and minimise form fields on site.

## 6. Outreach angle

Recognise the current website and describe a recurring-service reporting POC. Ask how supervisors prove scheduled work was completed and how client requests are captured. Offer a site dashboard and monthly report built around one real contract, using dummy data until approval.

### Draft first-contact message

> Hello, I reviewed Devine Facilities Management’s website and its landscaping, cleaning and plant-care offering. I would like to show you a practical site-care platform where supervisors can record scheduled work and site issues, while estate or property clients receive a simple dashboard and monthly service report. It would extend your existing digital presence into day-to-day service delivery. Could we discuss how your current site reporting works?

### Discovery questions

- Which services are most recurring and contract-based?
- How are site tasks scheduled and signed off?
- How do clients currently receive evidence of completed work?
- How are defects and client complaints assigned and tracked?
- Would a pilot on one estate or commercial property be practical?
- Which sustainability metrics are actually measured and could be reported?

## 7. Delivery plan and measurable outcomes

### Practical delivery phases

1. **Site workflow mapping** — Map client, site, service schedule, supervisor, evidence and escalation.
2. **Mobile POC** — Build task checklists, work logs and issue reporting for one service line.
3. **Client reporting pilot** — Create a client dashboard and monthly report for one site.
4. **Service expansion** — Add service lines, properties and optional performance metrics.

### Potential KPIs

- Scheduled tasks completed on time.
- Open issues past due.
- Time to acknowledge client requests.
- Client report preparation time.
- Contract retention and expansion opportunities.
